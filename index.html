Business Recommendations
01 · Score as Shared KPI
Use one score in CRM, campaign manager and personalization logic. Weekly migration between score bands should replace disconnected ad hoc engagement metrics.
02 · Churn Prevention Pipeline
Real-time churn scoring should fire only above 0.7. That threshold protects margin and still captures the majority of recoverable users.
03 · Cross-sell Targeting
Use the top 20% propensity cohort for financial-product campaigns, but keep message routing aligned to NBA category instead of a single broad cross-sell promo.
04 · Budget Reallocation
Shift spend toward FTravel and FBank. Both deliver better channel economics and better score quality than low-efficiency partner sources.
05 · App Adoption Goal
Users with three or more apps churn meaningfully less. Drive discovery with focused banners and first-order cashback rather than large universal bonus pools.
